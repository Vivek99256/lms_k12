# Scholar Compass — Product Overview

Last updated: 2026-11-04  
**Product:** Scholar Compass  
**Scope:** Career Guidance, Aptitude Intelligence & Future Pathways

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Scholar Compass** guides K-12 students through career exploration, stream selection, and aptitude discovery. Built on global occupational frameworks including **O*NET** and national education standards, Compass connects a student's academic performance, personality traits, and extracurricular interests with actionable future pathways.

---

## 2. Key Modules & Sub-systems

| Module | Purpose | Key Routes | Key Entities & Frameworks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Career Awareness** | Exploratory guides introducing students to evolving modern industries, emergent careers, and required competencies. | `/career-awareness`, `/career-counselling` | `career_clusters`, `industry_profiles` |
| **Career Explorer** | Interactive career discovery engine with multi-facet filters: education level, interests, work styles, and salary trends. | `/career-explorer`, `/career-explorer/dashboard` | `onet_occupations`, `onet_skills_taxonomy` |
| **Career Intelligence** | Psychometric evaluations, RIASEC interest profilers, and personalized stream recommendation reports (Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities). | `/career-intelligence`, `/career/recommendations` | `student_aptitude_scores`, `career_match_matrix` |
| **Counsellor Workbench** | Case management portal for school counsellors to review student assessments, schedule guidance sessions, and log advisory notes. | `/career-counselling/sessions`, `/career/counsellor-notes` | `counselling_logs`, `action_plans` |

---

## 3. Pedagogical & Career Alignment

Scholar Compass bridges the gap between classroom achievements in **Scholar Learn** and long-term career success:
- Evaluates subject-level affinities from Scholar Core marks.
- Integrates O*NET knowledge and skills taxonomies to recommend targeted vocational and academic pursuits.
- Empowers parents and mentors with concrete college pathway milestones.
